# Election window (Nov 3 midterm, inside our paper window) — worker B, cycle 19 (2026-10-22 ~12:55 ET)
Cross-refs: M-022/P-015 (midterm seasonality at 3–12 months), P-012/P-019 (live odds: House D ~92%, Senate D ~68%), P-006 (election-night repricing), MR-6 in areas/macro-regime.md.

## Key findings
- **Our exact window:** in midterm years the S&P averaged +1.85% from Oct 22 to Nov 5 (13/19 up). That beats presidential years (+1.33%) and odd years (+0.74%). t is ~1.8 and the last 4 midterms were mixed (EL-001).
- **Election days:** in midterm years the day before, election day and the day after tended to be up. The day after has ~1.5× normal absolute moves. In presidential years the day after is a high-variance coin flip (EL-002).

## Evidence strength
- C: n=14–19, price index only, and the NYSE was closed on some early election days.
- Consistent in direction with the longer-horizon midterm seasonality (M-022, P-015), which is B/C.

## What doesn't apply
- Presidential-year patterns: 2026 is a midterm.
- Party-outcome trades: the markets price a divided government as the base case (P-012). Only a surprise vs those odds would move prices (P-006).

## Rule candidates
- **EL-R1:** Stay invested in the core through Nov 3–4. Seasonality is mildly favorable and selling ahead of the election has no historical support (EL-001).
- **EL-R2:** Supports MR-6: no new single-name entries from 3 pm Nov 3 through the Nov 4 close, because of extra variance with no direction (EL-002).
- **EL-R3:** If the Senate outcome differs from the ~68% D favorite, treat Nov 4 as an EVENT day. Don't trade the first-hour reaction (EL-002, P-006, EH-004).

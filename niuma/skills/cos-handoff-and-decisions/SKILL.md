# CoS handoff and decisions

## Standing routing (approved by Wuyue)

Route without asking him first: photos go to photo, Canva and code go to 牛马, travel goes to zoom, and money or expenses go to Finance Bro. Tell the specialist to report straight to Wuyue, not back through CoS. Step in only when there's a decision for him to make. Don't relay back and forth.

## Handoffs to teammates

1. Assign a short task id (e.g. `cos-hilton-1`).
2. Message the teammate with: goal, acceptance criteria, constraints, the task id, and who to report to (Wuyue directly for routed specialists; otherwise CoS, including empty/none outcomes).
3. Tell Wuyue in one line who has it; then stay quiet.
4. When a teammate replies to CoS, match it to the id, update Notion Task Desk if needed, and relay only a decision, new result, or new failure.

Do not keep grinding travel, shopping, housing search, coaching, or first-line inbox work just because you have a login. Hand it off.

## Decisions

- One widget at a time. Cover: what it is, why now, real options, recommendation + one-line why.
- Consequential choices (money, returns, legal, sends): leave open on timeout/moved-on. Only an explicit choice or Skip closes it.
- Low-stakes optional widgets may use dismissOnMoveOn.

## Catch-me-up / ahoy

History-only. Recap concrete outcomes since the last real user message. List still-open decisions. Do not call live connectors just to freshen the recap. Do not invent state.

## Learning notes

After a teammate miss or inefficient path, SendToAgent a short note: what went wrong, what to do next time. Keep it diplomatic and actionable.

### 1. Use the FriesTrader shape: research after the close, verify the next morning, mechanical risk rules
- **Source.** The Xiaohongshu post by 抢走所有人的薯条 (physics PhD, FriesTrader developer), about 2026-08-07, with 3,379 likes and 162 comments.
- **What it does.** After the close, Claude writes theses with sources and confidence and places no orders. The next morning it re-verifies them, then runs position caps, stops, a daily loss limit and wash-sale checks. The AI can't override these. Everything is logged, and new users paper trade first.
- **Its track record.** The author's later posts report $500 at the start and a +2.2% first week. By month three the account was $1,538.82, up 2.33% that month, while QQQ fell 1.05% and VOO fell 0.01% (screenshots in the author's 2026-10-19 post). The week-8 post reported +1.62% against QQQ -0.15% (2026-09-18).
- **Why we picked it.** It is the only one of the three with a real process. It separates thinking from acting, has hard limits, keeps a log and starts on paper. A commenter (Hensen) also said the overnight re-check saved them from reversals.
- **Caveat.** The returns are small, short-run and self-reported, and the author says the approach can't be backtested.

### What we learned
- Holding the index is very hard to beat over a 2-week window. A random stock book beats both SPY and QQQ over 10 days only 21-27% of the time, and a SPY/QQQ mix always lands between the two.
- Momentum stock picking looks good on today's S&P 100 members (survivorship-biased), but unbiased Ken French data show no long-only large-cap momentum edge since 2009, so it stays paper-only.
- The current 2×ATR stop costs return on short holds; tight stops cut the momentum sanity backtest by about 15 pts/yr.
- Market timing (200-day average switches, sector rotation, valuation, going to cash around Fed or CPI days, reacting to down days) did not help in any test.
- Earnings drift (PEAD) is gone above microcaps after costs, and buying an earnings gap-up near the open is buying the move that already happened.
- A quality junk veto mainly avoids blow-ups: failing names were 3 to 8 times more likely to lose half their value in 12 months.
- For small-cap news trades, buying after an attention spike loses on average, and our own two tests found no edge in buying early either.
- On a $1,000 Robinhood cash account: fractional orders are market-only, whole shares (1 VOO + 1 QQQM) fit a 70/30 mix, and sale proceeds settle T+1.

5. **Loss breakers measured on our own account (HOT 14).** A -2% day (close vs prior close) means no new buys that day; -8% from the equity high means no new buys and a review with Wuyue, with no forced selling. 6. **Tradable test replaces the $10B market-cap minimum.** Default: NYSE/Nasdaq, price ≥ $5, market cap ≥ $300M, ADV ≥ $2M, spread ≤ 0.5%, a 10-K/10-Q within 150 days, no bankruptcy or late filings, ≥ 8 quarters of revenue (plus the profit gate below $3.66B, the hype exclusions and the information checklist). Why: listing, price, volume and spread decide whether a small order fills fairly; company size doesn't.
8. **"Beat the index" defined.** Each weekly retro shows us against the better of SPY and QQQ that week, but performance is judged on the cumulative return since 2026-10-22 against SPY, QQQ and a 70/30 SPY/QQQ blend, after costs.

# Daily schedule (weekdays, US market days)

| Time (ET) | Step | Output |
|---|---|---|
| ~9:45am | Re-verify yesterday's proposals with fresh prices and news; apply risk limits; log paper fills | Notion trade log + day file |
| ~4:30pm | After-close market brief (sourced, @tychozzz format) with ideas and warnings | Notion research section + day file |
| ~6:30pm | Candidates for tomorrow: thesis, sources, confidence 1–5, paper size | Notion + day file |
| after the day's steps | Commit the day file to this repo | `days/YYYY-MM-DD.md` |

No step places a real order. During paper trading (through 2026-11-05) fills are paper only. Market holidays are skipped.

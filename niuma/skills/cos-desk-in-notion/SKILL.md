# CoS Desk in Notion

Use this to keep open work and weekly priorities in Notion, not in chat.

## Pages

- **Task Desk**: system of record for open work.
- **CoS Desk**: two parts:
  1. Weekly stack: 3–5 priorities, rewritten every Sunday, plus a short "won't do" list.
  2. Decision log: append-only. When a decision lands, add what was decided. Do not edit old rows to rewrite history.

## Rules

- Dedup on intake. If the user hands over a todo that already exists (same outcome, even if the wording differs), update the existing row instead of creating a near-duplicate.
- Morning: score mail and calendar against the CoS Desk stack. Do not dump the whole inbox onto Task Desk.
- Evening: capture new decisions and real actions onto the desks. Stay quiet if nothing new landed.
- Link the desks in any ping that created or closed work.

## If Notion is missing

Do not block. Keep the same rules in chat for that run, and create or update the desks as soon as Notion is connected.

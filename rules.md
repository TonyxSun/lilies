## Mechanical risk limits
These are applied as written. A strong story never overrides them. They are default values the system proposed on 2026-10-22, not taken from any source, and Wuyue can change them.

- At most 10% of the account in any one stock, and at most 12 positions.
- Cash only. No margin, no shorting, no options.
- Stop loss at 2× the 14-day ATR below entry, never wider than -8%. After entry it trails the highest close since entry.
- If the account loses 2% in a day, no new positions that day. If it falls 8% from its high, everything pauses for a review with Wuyue.
- Never add to a losing position.
- Wash sale: after selling at a loss, don't buy the same stock back for 30 days.
- No new position within 2 trading days before earnings.
- Screen: price above its 200-day moving average and market cap above $10B.

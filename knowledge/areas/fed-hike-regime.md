# Fed hike regime — worker B, cycle 13 (2026-10-22 ~12:46 ET)
Cross-refs: P-008/P-019 (market-implied path), P-011 (Bernanke–Kuttner surprise response), M-020 (macro calendar).

## Key findings
- **We are already in a tightening phase.** FRED shows a hike to 4.00% upper bound on 2026-09-17, after the 2024–25 cuts. Today is day 25 (FH-001). Prediction markets price the next hike for Dec 9 (~76%) and a hold on Oct 28 (~93%).
- **History after first hikes:** the S&P fell in the first month in all 6 cycles from 1994 to 2022, and 2026 broke that (+0.3%). It was weak at 3 months (1 of 6 up) and normal by 6 months (FH-002).
- **Our exact window (days 25–35):** coin flip for the S&P; Nasdaq slightly worse, 2 of 6 up (FH-003).
- **Closest analogs** (1997, 1999 re-hikes after cuts) disagree in direction (FH-004).

## Evidence strength
- FH-001 is A (official data).
- The event study is C: n=6, overlapping macro shocks (2022 inflation, 2015 China/oil, 1999 dot-com), and index-level only.

## What doesn't apply
- Rate-cut-cycle playbooks (2024–25 "cuts are bullish" narratives) are stale now.
- Sector rotation by rate regime is owned by another worker (sector-rotation.md), so it is not duplicated here.

## Rule candidates
- **FH-R1:** Label the macro regime "early tightening (first hike 2026-09-17)" in every daily plan. Any thesis built on falling rates needs explicit justification (FH-001).
- **FH-R2:** No extra growth or Nasdaq overweight beyond the QQQ benchmark weight on rate grounds. Historical post-first-hike windows lean against long-duration equity, but too weakly to act on the other way either (FH-002/003).
- **FH-R3:** The Dec 9 hike is ~76% priced. Only a *surprise* versus that price moves stocks (P-011), so don't trade on the expectation of the hike itself.

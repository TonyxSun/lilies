# Insider trades (Form 4): worth adding? (owner 牛马; new area, item e; 12:55pm ET). Verdict: cold.
- **Literature:** the signal is concentrated in small firms and is weak in large caps (Lakonishok-Lee 2001). Only opportunistic, non-routine trades are informative (Cohen-Malloy-Pomorski 2012) (IN-001, B).
- **After 2012:** reported decay. A 2014–26 pre-registered null shows +0.85% over 63 days with a negative median, but this is UNVERIFIED because the fetch timed out (IN-002, C).
- **Fit for the project:** poor. We hold large caps over 2 weeks, and the documented effects work on months-long horizons in small caps.
- **Rule IT-R1:** don't use Form 4 purchases as a buy trigger. A large opportunistic *sale* cluster could be logged as context only. Untested; this is not a rule.

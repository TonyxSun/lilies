## Key findings
- **Semis barely react to single hyperscaler prints.** The reporter moves ~5.5% vs SPY, but SMH moves only ~1.4% vs ~1.15% on a normal day, with no consistent sign (RT-001).
- **The first reporter tells you nothing about the rest.** Same sign 11/23 seasons, corr -0.18 (RT-002).
- **Today's narrative is not a forecast.** "AI capex doubts" after META is a story, not information about the MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN prints (RT-003).

## Evidence strength
- B: SEC dates are exact, but this uses only the "recent" filings block (META from 2024-07, GOOGL from 2023-07). n=70 events and 23 seasons. Single-day closes.

## What doesn't apply

## Rule candidates
- **RT-R1:** Don't pre-position (buy or sell) MSFT, GOOGL or AMZN, or semis, based on how META or another earlier reporter reacted (RT-002).
- **RT-R2:** Treat each of MSFT/GOOGL (Oct 28) and AMZN (Oct 29) as an independent event of roughly ±5%. Size with EH-R1. Don't hedge one with another (RT-001/002).
- **RT-R3:** "Narrative selloffs" in semis around hyperscaler earnings don't change the core plan (DD-R1, RT-003).
